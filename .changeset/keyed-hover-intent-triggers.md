Let one HoverIntent serve many triggers that share a panel, instead of one HoverIntent per trigger that closes and reopens the panel as the pointer moves between them. Spread `RenderInfo.keyedTrigger(key)` onto each trigger and render the panel for `RenderInfo.maybeActiveTriggerKey`. Moving between triggers while the panel is open hands it to the new trigger at once, and only the latest trigger's open wait can open it. HoverIntent tracks the pointer and focus separately. When the active trigger loses one of them, the panel waits the usual close grace period, then moves to another trigger that still has the pointer or focus, or closes. Escape on a trigger dismisses the panel for the trigger that receives it, and also for the active trigger when the panel is open. A dismissed trigger does not take the panel, even while the pointer or focus passes over it, until the pointer and focus have both left it and, while the panel belongs to that trigger, the panel. Each trigger keeps its own dismissal. The new `EnteredKeyedTrigger`, `LeftKeyedTrigger`, `FocusedKeyedTrigger`, `BlurredKeyedTrigger`, and `PressedEscapeOnKeyedTrigger` Messages carry the trigger's `key`. When a keyed trigger's element is removed, send the new `InformedOfRemovedKeyedTrigger` InMessage with its key, for example through the Binding's `informOfRemovedKeyedTrigger({ key })`, so HoverIntent forgets the pointer, focus, and dismissal on it. The unkeyed `trigger` bundle and its Messages behave as before, including how leave and blur events and Escape dismissal interact with the panel.

This is a breaking change. These public types change:

- The HoverIntent Model replaces `isTriggerHovered: boolean` with `maybeHoveredTrigger: Option<TriggerEngagement>`, where `TriggerEngagement` is `{ trigger: Trigger, isDismissed: boolean }`, and adds `activeTrigger: Trigger`. `isPanelHovered: boolean` becomes `maybeHoveredPanel: Option<PanelEngagement>`, where `PanelEngagement` is `{ isDismissed: boolean }`. `isDismissed` is removed from the Model; Escape dismissal is now the `isDismissed` flag on the hovered and focused trigger and on the hovered and focused panel. `maybeFocusLocation` now holds a `FocusLocation` value, `FocusLocation.Trigger({ trigger, isDismissed })` or `FocusLocation.Panel({ isDismissed })`, instead of the strings `'Trigger'` and `'Panel'`. `Trigger` is `Trigger.Unkeyed()` for the `trigger` bundle or `Trigger.Keyed({ key })` for `keyedTrigger(key)`. `@foldkit/ui/hoverIntent` exports the `Trigger`, `TriggerEngagement`, `PanelEngagement`, and `FocusLocation` Schemas. `init` sets `activeTrigger` to `Trigger.Unkeyed()` and the three Option fields to `Option.none()`. Code that builds a HoverIntent Model by hand, such as a test fixture, needs these fields. Code that reads `isTriggerHovered` or `isPanelHovered` can check `Option.isSome` on `maybeHoveredTrigger` or `maybeHoveredPanel`, and code that reads `isDismissed` can check the `isDismissed` flags on those records and on `maybeFocusLocation`.
- The HoverIntent Message union has the five keyed variants above, and the InMessage union has `InformedOfRemovedKeyedTrigger`. Exhaustive matches on either union need to handle them.
