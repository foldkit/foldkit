# Stabilization evidence

The [release preparation report](../preview-stabilization-20261027.md) interprets
these retained results. Implementation is pinned to
`ae2eba84283939f5991a1b0c35c32ba351f9efdc`; the published comparison is
`f3be22cfeefed437164ad614d08b16d9000e0703` and integrated main is
`b702a5efbb74bebc056b798b414ddb923a82fbd5`.

- `verification.json` records the final 15 integrated gates, checker-order
  controls, metadata qualifiers and log hashes. Earlier failed harness attempts
  are excluded from its final gate list and remain in the original evidence.
- `package-rehearsal.json` is a compact extraction of the complete rehearsal:
  nine local package identities, installed consumer contracts, browser outcomes
  and unchanged actual Coverchart. `rehearsal-execution.json` records recovery
  steps; `coverchart-age-transition.json` records the exact package-age handling.
- `workspace-declarations.json` records the final ordinary-root strict relocated
  consumer proof. `declaration-controls.json` and the three patches preserve
  the failed alternatives and retained boundary against exact
  `8b4180822316edd736809ab65136c850be488281`. Do not apply these experiment
  patches to the final candidate. A failed encoding is not a universal
  TypeScript limitation.
- `input-boundary-mutations.json` records disposable JavaScript mutations and
  restoration. The committed Route/URL tests and source/emitted fixtures retain
  the executable regressions.
- `daybreak-initial.txt`, `daybreak-addendum.txt` and `independent-review.txt`
  preserve the exact original review text. Their distinct source pins and
  limits remain authoritative. Later evidence commits do not extend static
  security review to unreviewed source changes.
- `feedback-recheck.json` supplements the exhaustive source-linked
  [feedback inventory](../stabilization-feedback-20261027.md).
- `performance.json` retains all 12 native runs and 36 rendering samples,
  request timings, RSS sampling, exact source/tool/driver hashes, paired
  statistics and source/bundle-preservation checks, without full LSP responses.

Original local evidence directories retain logs and larger artifacts. Historical
absolute paths identify those runs; they are not public installation paths.
Retained JSON may be normalized by the repository formatter. The evidence
manifest distinguishes original source bytes from retained bytes. No registry
publication or deployment acceptance is represented by a local tarball result.
